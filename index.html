Pacman-Clone

A 2 local player small solo game made with a randomized map everytime you play

About

This was part of a portfolio building project my college had, we needed to create a small game with something that pushes your skills. I decided to mess around with random map generation, I also made it a 2 player only game (Pacman and a Ghost). Both players have their own special abilitys they can use as an advantage to win the game.

Process

Art/Visuals: I found some free sprite sheets for Pacman and the Ghost (along with other assets), which made it easier for me to focus on the technical side of things.

Sound/Music: I got all the audio online to, again, make it easier for me.

Code: The main mechanic (random map generation) was quite difficult and took a while to perfect, reading forums writing and removing code for weeks. In the end the map generation code isn't perfect, but I'm still proud of what I managed to do in such a small amount of time.
